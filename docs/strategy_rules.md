# SMC Sniper — Full Strategy Rules

## Core Concept

Top-down CRT-based reversal strategy. The Daily CRT sweep is the ONLY reason to be in a trade.
Everything below the Daily is confirmation and precision.

---

## Entry Sequence

### Step 1 — Daily: CRT Sweep + DFVG
- CRT High Sweep = price sweeps above prior Daily High → BEARISH bias
- CRT Low Sweep = price sweeps below prior Daily Low → BULLISH bias
- Confirm DFVG rejection on the same daily close
- No sweep = no trade setup below

### Step 2 — 4H: FVG + Major S&R
- Mark the 4H FVG in direction of bias
- Identify major horizontal S&R levels (4H/Daily pivot highs and lows)
- These are FLAT horizontal lines — not trendlines
- A rejection FROM a major S&R level = strong confluence
- Levels that have 3+ touches are the most significant

### Step 3 — 1H: MSS + Order Block
- Wait for 1H MSS (Market Structure Shift) after Daily sweep
- Bearish MSS = price breaks below recent 1H Higher Low
- Bullish MSS = price breaks above recent 1H Lower High
- Mark 1H OB at the MSS origin candle
- No MSS = still waiting, do NOT enter

### Step 4 — 30m: Refined OB + Killzone
- Find 30m OB nested inside the 1H OB
- Confirm inside valid ICT Killzone:
  - London: 02:00–05:00 UTC
  - NY AM: 07:00–10:00 UTC
  - NY PM: 13:00–16:00 UTC
  - Asian = SKIP

### Step 5 — 15m: Entry Candle
- Price taps DFVG or OB zone
- Rejection candle: strong wick (>60% of range), engulfing, or pin bar
- Enter on candle close or next open
- SL: above/below the OB
- TP: next liquidity level / opposite CRTH or CRTL

---

## Invalidation Rules

- No Daily CRT sweep = no setup exists
- No 1H MSS after sweep = wait
- Asian session = skip
- DFVG already fully mitigated = invalid
- 1H OB fully engulfed on close = exit
- Price already moved 60–80% from zone = do not chase

---

## Trade Management

- Entry: 15m rejection candle close
- SL: above/below 1H OB (30m OB for tighter)
- TP1: 50% at nearest liquidity
- TP2: full at CRTH/CRTL opposite level
- Move SL to BE after TP1
